## Description

WaveLens makes it easier to capture the sessions you will never forget.

Discover approved local surf photographers, videographers, and drone creators near your favorite breaks. Browse their services, portfolios, travel range, and starting hourly rate, then send a booking request with the session details that matter.

With WaveLens, you can:

- Find local surf photo, video, and drone creators
- Browse creator profiles, travel range, services, and portfolios
- Compare starting hourly rates
- Request a surf session for a specific date and session length
- Share the surf spot and your preferred contact method directly with the creator
- Track whether your request is pending, accepted, or declined
- Apply to join WaveLens as a surf creator

Creators can review incoming requests and respond from their WaveLens dashboard.

WaveLens does not process payments in the current version. Payment is arranged only after a creator accepts a request.

Users can permanently delete their account and associated booking data from the Account screen.
