# WaveLens build 11

Build 11 was uploaded to TestFlight and verified on a physical iPhone on August 8, 2026.

## What changed

- Added a session-details screen from customer and creator booking lists.
- Added customer cancellation and creator Accept/Decline actions.
- Kept Messages and Delivery Gallery accessible from each session.
- Improved keyboard dismissal and booking-form scrolling.
- Corrected iPhone status-bar backgrounds and Back-button spacing.
- Aligned creator pages on desktop.
- Corrected production password-reset redirects.

## Verified flows

- Sign up, email confirmation, sign in, sign out, and password reset.
- Manual spot selection and foreground location permission.
- Customer request creation, Pending history, and account isolation.
- Creator request visibility, Accept, and Decline status handoff.
- Customer cancellation, Messages, and Delivery Gallery navigation.
- Account deletion and rejected sign-in after deletion.

## Tester note

Focus on booking navigation and layout. Open Account, choose Booking requests, open a session, and confirm its actions match your role. Report the screen, action, expected result, actual result, and a screenshot when possible.

Build 11 is a follow-up TestFlight build. It does not replace or modify version 1.0 while Apple review is pending.
